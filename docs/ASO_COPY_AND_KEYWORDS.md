3. Full Description (Google Play Optimized)

Help your baby learn to self-soothe and get a full night's rest with the simplest Ferber Method sleep training timer on Google Play.

Designed specifically for exhausted parents, this straightforward interval timer takes the stress and guesswork out of baby sleep training. Follow Dr. Richard Ferber’s gradual extinction approach with automated progressive intervals, clear check-in notifications, and zero clutter.

KEY FEATURES:

• Automated Ferber Intervals: Pre-configured check-in timers according to standard Ferber sleep training schedules (Day 1 through Day 7+).
• One-Tap Timer Controls: Start, pause, or adjust check-in countdowns effortlessly in the dark during bedtime or naptime.
• Custom Schedules: Easily customize interval times to match your pediatrician's advice or your baby's unique sleep routine.
• Live Interval Logging: Log crying duration, check-in responses, and total time to fall asleep to track bedtime progress over time.
• Night-Mode Friendly UI: Soft colors and dark-mode themes designed to protect your vision during middle-of-the-night sleep sessions.
• Distraction-Free Design: Clean interface with no complex setups or disruptive ads during critical sleep checks.

WHY USE THE FERBER METHOD FOR BABY SLEEP?
The Ferber Method (gradual extinction) helps infants and toddlers develop healthy sleep habits through predictable interval check-ins. Consistent timing is critical to success. Our app acts as your reliable bedtime companion, tracking exact interval lengths so you don’t have to do mental math while tired.

WHETHER FOR NAPTIME OR NIGHTTIME:
Track both daily nap routines and evening sleep sessions. View clear history trends to see how rapidly your baby’s self-soothing improves day by day.
