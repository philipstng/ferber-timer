What it is

The Ferber method, sometimes called "graduated check-ins," is one approach some families use to help a baby learn to fall asleep independently. The baby is put down drowsy but awake and the parent leaves the room. If the baby cries, the parent waits a set amount of time, then makes a brief, calm visit before leaving again. The waits get gradually longer.

How a night can go

  1. Follow your usual bedtime routine, then place your baby down drowsy but awake.
  2. Say goodnight, leave the room, and start the timer.
  3. When it ends, if your baby is still upset, go in for a brief check-in of about 1–2 minutes. Speak softly and offer reassurance. The method generally avoids picking the baby up.
  4. Leave and start the next timer, which will be a little longer.
  5. Repeat until your baby falls asleep. Many versions of the method restart the waits from the first interval after a night waking, but your pediatrician may suggest something different.

Using this timer

  • Day is which day of the plan you are on (1–7). Later days use longer waits.
  • Check-In Interval is which wait you are on: 1st, 2nd, 3rd, then "Subseq." for every wait after the third. When a timer ends, the next interval is selected for you.
  • Tap Start when you leave the room. When time is up, the app sounds an alarm and sends a notification.
  • After a check-in, just tap Start again. To use a different interval, tap it first.
  • Script is optional. Write something calm to say or read to your baby, and open it while the timer runs. You can tap Edit to change it at any time, even while the timer is running.
  • Day and interval are locked while the timer runs. Tap Pause to change them. Reset appears next to Resume (or Start, after the alarm) and sets the timer back to the full interval.
  • The speaker icon cycles the alarm between sound with vibration, vibration only, and silent. The notification follows the same setting. Do Not Disturb can still silence it, because it is a notification and not a clock alarm.
  • The half-circle / moon / sun icon cycles the look between light, dark, and a very dim warm night view.
  • The clock icon opens the night log, which records each timer automatically so you can see how a night went.
